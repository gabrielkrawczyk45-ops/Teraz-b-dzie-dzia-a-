Grammar Academy

Present Simple

Use: routines, facts, regular actions.
I work 12-hour shifts.
I go to college on Wednesdays.
He works nights.

Present Continuous

Use: now, temporary situations, arrangements.
I am studying English now.
I am working tonight.
We are meeting at 11:30.
1. I ___ in Wakefield.
2. I ___ tonight.
3. She ___ English every Wednesday.
Wynik pojawi się tutaj.

Past Simple

Yesterday I worked a night shift.
Last weekend I went to the gym.
I didn't work on Monday.
Did you go to college?
1. Last weekend I ___ at work.
2. Did you ___ to the gym?
3. I didn't ___ yesterday.

Present Perfect

have/has + past participle
I have lived in the UK for 11 years.
I have worked here since 2023.
Have you ever visited Scotland?

for = period: for 11 years. since = starting point: since 2015.

1. I ___ lived in the UK for 11 years.
2. I have worked here ___ 2023.
3. I have studied English ___ two months.

Used to / Would

I used to live in Poland.
When I was a child, I would play outside every day.
Did you use to play football?
I didn't use to drink coffee.

Used to works for past states and repeated actions. Would is mainly for repeated past actions, not states.

1. I ___ be shy.
2. Did you ___ play football?
3. Every summer we ___ visit our grandparents.

Question forms

Do you work nights?
Did you go yesterday?
Have you finished?
Are you working tonight?
Where do you live?
1. ___ you work nights?
2. ___ you working tonight?
3. ___ you ever visited London?

ROUTINE / FACT
I go / I live / I work
Present Simple — rutyna, fakt, coś regularnego.

I go to the gym three times a week.
I live in Wakefield.
I work 12-hour shifts.

NOW / ARRANGEMENT
I am going / I am working
Present Continuous — teraz, tymczasowo lub zaplanowane.

I am going to the gym now.
I am working tonight.
I am meeting her tomorrow.

PAST
I went / I worked / I lived
Past Simple — zakończona czynność w przeszłości.

I went to the gym yesterday.
I worked last weekend.
I lived in Poland before I moved to the UK.

PAST HABIT
I used to go
Used to — kiedyś robiłem / byłem, ale teraz już niekoniecznie.

I used to go to the gym after work.
I used to live in Poland.
I used to be shy.

I go vs I am going — bardzo ważne

❌ I am go — tego NIE używamy.
Po am / is / are czasownik zwykle musi mieć końcówkę -ing:
✅ I am going.
✅ She is working.
✅ We are studying.

I go = robię regularnie / zwykle

Słowa podpowiadające: now, right now, at the moment, today, tonight, this week, tomorrow.

A co z „I am living”?

I live in Wakefield. = to jest mój normalny, stały fakt.
I am living with my brother this month. = sytuacja tymczasowa.
I have lived in the UK for 11 years. = zaczęło się w przeszłości i trwa do teraz.

Szybka reguła do zapamiętania

EVERY / USUALLY / OFTEN → I go / I work / I study
NOW / TODAY / TONIGHT / AT THE MOMENT → I am going / I am working / I am studying
YESTERDAY / LAST WEEK → I went / I worked / I studied
FOR / SINCE + do teraz → I have lived / I have worked / I have studied

Used to czy Would?

USED TO
Możesz użyć do czynności i stanów:
✅ I used to play football.
✅ I used to be shy.
✅ I used to live in Poland.
WOULD
Głównie do powtarzających się czynności:
✅ When I was a child, I would play outside every day.
❌ I would be shy. (jako zwykły stan w przeszłości — lepiej used to)

GO family

went = poszedłem / pojechałem → Past Simple

used = używał / użyty

WOULD

would = by / zrobiłbym; także dawny powtarzalny zwyczaj

Reading Comprehension

College notice

English Entry 3 classes take place every Wednesday from 9:00 a.m. to 12:45 p.m. Students should arrive ten minutes early and bring a notebook, a pen and their course folder. If you cannot attend, contact the college before the lesson. During the final month, students will complete practice assessments to prepare for the exam.
1. What day are the classes?
2. What time should students arrive?
3. Why will students do practice assessments?
Travel message

Hi Anna, our train leaves Leeds at 7:40 on Saturday morning. We should get to the station by 7:15 because we need time to buy coffee and find the correct platform. The journey to Manchester takes just over an hour. When we arrive, let's check in at the hotel first and then walk into the city centre.
1. What time does the train leave?
2. Why should they arrive early?
3. What will they do first in Manchester?
Workplace update

Production will stop at 2:00 p.m. on Friday for planned cleaning and maintenance. All operators must finish the current product before the shutdown and leave their work areas clean and safe. Engineering will start work at 2:30 p.m. The line is expected to restart at 6:00 p.m., but supervisors should check with Engineering before starting production.
1. Why will production stop?
2. When will Engineering start?
3. What must supervisors do before restart?

My Common Mistakes — specjalnie pod Twoje błędy

WrongBetterWhy
❌ I am living in UK for 11 years.✅ I have lived in the UK for 11 years.Present Perfect for a situation from the past until now.
❌ I starting work at 6 am.✅ I start work at 6 a.m.Routine → Present Simple.
❌ In last weekend I was at work.✅ Last weekend I was at work.No “in” before last weekend.
❌ After get out from work...✅ After I finished work... / After work...Use a complete clause or natural phrase.
❌ I like walking with dog around the park nearly my house.✅ I like walking my dog in the park near my house.Natural word order and “near”, not “nearly”.
❌ I feeling good.✅ I feel good. / I am feeling good.You need the correct verb form.
❌ We going to coffee shop.✅ We are going to a coffee shop.Missing “are” and article “a”.
